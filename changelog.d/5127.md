### Fixed

- **Saved ListBox and ComboBox records load the measured VB6 values** (part of #5127). Missing ItemData values default to zero, extra entries and trailing bytes are ignored, and leading decimal data wraps as a 32-bit Long. Truncated List records report VB6's property error; unmeasured cases still refuse, naming #5127. Style 2 ComboBoxes deliberately keep every saved item without ItemData instead of reproducing VB6's first-item loss.

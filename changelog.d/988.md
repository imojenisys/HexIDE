### Added

- **Forms and PictureBoxes draw through their own coordinate systems** (#988). Line, PSet, Cls and Point now use a retained bitmap behind the controls, with measured pen-width-one pixels, boxes, colours and current-position rounding. Drawing state loads saved values and is readable at run time. Both grammars recognise graphics syntax, including Circle; execution of circles, thick pens, other drawing modes and late-bound graphics remains explicitly unimplemented.
